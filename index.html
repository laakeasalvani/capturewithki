About Ki

nine years of watching light

Khiara Netherly

I shoot for the ten-years-from-now version of you

Trends age. The way your grandmother held your hand before the ceremony does not. I photograph with that in mind — editorial in composition, quiet in approach, so the day stays yours while I work around the edges of it.

Most of my couples tell me the same thing afterward: they forgot I was there. That’s the goal. I direct when direction helps, and otherwise I stay out of the way and pay attention.

Based in Honolulu, and happiest on a plane to somewhere new. Travel is welcome and often my favorite part of the job.

Good to know
Do you travel?

Often. Travel within Oʻahu is included. Neighbor islands and mainland weddings are quoted per trip — usually flights, one to two nights of lodging, and ground transit at cost.

Can we split payments?

Yes. After the retainer, the balance can be spread across monthly payments up to two weeks before the date.

What if we’re awkward in front of a camera?

Nearly everyone says this. I direct enough to keep you comfortable and then let you be, which is where the photos you actually want come from.

How many images do we get?

Roughly 60–80 edited images per hour of coverage. Every one is color-corrected by hand — no batch presets, no AI edits.
