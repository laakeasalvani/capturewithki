Behind the camera

About Ki

Khiara Salvani

I shoot for the ten-years-from-now version of you

I love this job. Every session is a new group of people who trust me with a day that matters to them, and I don’t take that lightly — or too seriously, either. My favorite moments are the ones nobody planned: the laugh before the vows, the little kid stealing the spotlight, the look between two people who forgot anyone else was watching.

My couples usually tell me the same thing afterward: they had way more fun than they expected, and somehow forgot I was even there. That’s exactly what I’m going for. I direct when it helps, and otherwise I hang back, stay ready, and let the good stuff happen.

Based in Honolulu, and always up for a trip somewhere new. Travel is welcome and one of my favorite parts of the job.

Good to know
Do you travel?

Often. Travel within Oʻahu is included. Neighbor islands and mainland weddings are quoted per trip — usually flights, one to two nights of lodging, and ground transit at cost.

Can we split payments?

Yes. After the retainer, the balance can be spread across monthly payments up to two weeks before the date.

What if we’re awkward in front of a camera?

Nearly everyone says this. I direct enough to keep you comfortable and then let you be, which is where the photos you actually want come from.

How many images do we get?

Delivery counts are listed with each collection above — every image is color-corrected by hand, no batch presets, no AI edits.
